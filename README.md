### 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes Deskify’s office supply sales data from 2009 to 2012 to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, and regional trends. Using Power BI, an interactive dashboard was built to provide stakeholders with a centralized view of key business metrics across multiple years.
The analysis supports better inventory planning, customer segmentation, and logistics optimization by highlighting high-performing product categories, loyal customers, and profitable regions.

### 🎯 Business Problem
Deskify needed clearer visibility into:
- Which product categories and sub-categories drive profit
- How customer segments contribute to overall profitability
- Regional differences in sales and profit performance
- The effectiveness of different delivery modes
Without integrated reporting, decision-makers lacked actionable insights to optimise product strategy, customer focus, and distribution planning.

### 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI for interactive dashboards and visual analytics
- Power Query for data cleaning and transformation
- DAX for KPIs such as total sales, profit, and profit margin
- Map and Tree visuals for regional and hierarchical analysis

### 🗂 Data Description
The dataset covers office supply sales transactions from 2009 to 2012 and includes:
- Product details across Furniture, Technology, and Office Supplies
- Customer information and customer segment classification
- Order level sales and profit data
- Shipping and delivery mode information
- Geographic data for regional and state-level analysis
The data model enables multi-dimensional analysis across time, product, customer, and region.

### 📈 Dashboard Scope & Features
- Overview KPIs showing total sales, total profit, total orders, and average margin
- Category Analysis comparing sales and profit across Furniture, Technology, and Office Supplies
- Regional Profit Map highlighting top-performing states
- Customer Insights ranks top customers by total profit
- Delivery Mode Analysis visualizing order distribution by shipping method

### 🔍 Key Insights
-Office Supplies generated the largest share of total revenue across the period
- Furniture followed closely in profitability, indicating strong margins
- Corporate and Consumer customer segments delivered the highest profits
- A small group of customers contributed disproportionately to overall profit
- Regular Air shipping accounted for most deliveries, reflecting operational efficiency

### ✅ Conclusion
The Deskify Power BI dashboard provides a centralized analytics solution that enables management to track multi-year performance, evaluate profitability drivers, and understand customer behaviour. The dashboard replaces static reporting with interactive insights to enable faster, more informed decision-making.

### 🚀 Recommendations
- Prioritise high-margin product categories when planning inventory and promotions
- Strengthen relationships with top profit-generating customers
- Use regional profit insights to guide market expansion and sales focus
- Continue leveraging efficient delivery modes to control logistics costs
- Monitor category and customer trends over time to support forecasting and planning
